Primary request: Create the female anthropomorphization of Graham’s Union Flag Custom (SVMS-01E) from Mobile Suit Gundam 00 as a clearly adult woman in her late twenties. The character concept is a cold, provocative high-speed ace: elegant, self-assured, obsessive about winning, and visibly built around the machine’s thin aerodynamic silhouette rather than generic bulky Gundam armor.

SUBJECT AND IDENTITY
A very tall, long-legged, lean athletic adult woman with narrow shoulders, a sharply defined waist, and graceful but battle-ready proportions. Warm light-olive skin. A distinctive elongated oval face with high cheekbones, a clean tapered jaw, a straight narrow nose, slightly full lips, and sharp almond-shaped amber-gold eyes. Her expression is a restrained, challenging half-smile with intense competitive focus. Raven-black chin-length asymmetric bob hair with a subtle gunmetal-blue sheen, deep side part, and one long swept fringe; face fully visible. Do not resemble the canonical pilot.

MECHA TRANSLATION
Faithfully preserve the recognizable design language and canonical equipment of Graham’s black Union Flag Custom: extremely slim black and charcoal-gray aerodynamic armor, narrow pointed chest and waist forms, long blade-like leg armor, compact angular shoulder shells, silver-gray structural edges, small restrained orange-red sensor accents, and crisp aircraft-panel construction. The armor should be high coverage but sleek, fitted, and subtly alluring through silhouette, with small controlled skin exposure at the upper arms and side waist only—no nudity.
Exactly two normal human arms and exactly two normal human legs, anatomically correct, with intact human hands and feet. Keep compact wing and flight-control surfaces mounted to the back as external mechanical equipment; they must not replace limbs or become organic wings. Preserve the modified linear rifle as a long, narrow, purposeful weapon, the defense rod as compact forearm-mounted defensive equipment, and the sonic blade as recognizable secondary equipment. No extra arms, no extra legs, no aircraft nose replacing the torso, no helmet, no mask.

COMPOSITION
Vertical 2:3. Tight intentional full-body framing at the largest practical scale, complete crown-to-feet view, minimal empty margin. Keep all major back equipment and the long rifle inside the frame without making the figure unnecessarily small. Let the model choose a natural dynamic pose and cinematic view angle; do not force a fixed pose or fixed camera direction. Face, both hands, and both feet must remain readable.

SCENE AND LIGHTING
A restrained dark aircraft maintenance hangar with a smooth floor, faint structural lights, and minimal visual clutter. Cool steel-blue key light, narrow warm amber rim light, and subtle atmospheric depth. The character must remain the dominant subject.

STYLE
High-end painterly semi-realistic character illustration with naturalistic cinematic lighting and restrained anime-informed facial idealization. Mature believable adult facial anatomy, dimensional painted skin, grouped painterly hair masses with selected fine strands, convincing mechanical materials rendered in the same painterly language, selective sharpness, and controlled reflections. The result must read as a sophisticated semi-realistic digital painting, not a live-action photograph, not a 3D render, not glossy mobile-game 2.5D art, and not cel-shaded anime.

Constraints: no text, no logos, no typography, no pilot likeness, no childlike proportions, no cropped feet, no duplicated limbs, no organic wings, no generic Gundam V-fin face motif, no gore.
